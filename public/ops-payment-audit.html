Payment Audit

Every won-stage deal with the real Xero invoices found for that customer — whether it's a 50% deposit + 50% final split or 100% upfront is a manual call on your side with nothing recorded in Pipely, so this doesn't guess which one applies. It just lays out what's actually in Xero (matched by customer, not just our own invoice tags) so you can eyeball whether it looks right during a scan. "Deal value" is Pipely's own field and is often $0/unreliable — treat "Xero invoices" as the real numbers.
