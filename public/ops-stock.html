Stock on Shore — Silverdale

Physically in the warehouse right now — "Available"/"Balance" are against the landed batch only (In Stock minus that batch's reservations/new orders), matching the sheet's own original formula. The "Incoming (on the water)" column is separate — units already pre-committed against later batch shipments that haven't landed yet, so they don't reduce what's on shore today. A red/negative on-shore balance means more is committed against the CURRENT warehouse stock than physically exists — check Products To Order on the Dashboard.
